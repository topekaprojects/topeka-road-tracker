ABOUT THIS COMMUNITY PROJECT

Built by a Topeka resident who got tired of guessing.

This site was created by Joseph Romero to make road projects, closures, weather, pothole reporting, news and events easier to find and understand.

JR
JOSEPH ROMERO • TOPEKA, KANSAS

Why I made the tracker.

I’m a Topeka resident who is tired of popping tires on potholes and being late because of road closures. I wanted one place where people could quickly see construction projects, road conditions, weather, events, local news, and the tools to report problems.
