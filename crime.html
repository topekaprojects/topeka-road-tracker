CRIME RATE & SUMMARY

Understanding Topeka crime data

Crime rate means reported offenses divided by population, typically expressed per 100,000 residents. A reliable figure must specify the year, geography, offenses included, reporting methodology and population estimate.

Current verified crime rate: Not displayed until a dated official dataset is available. We do not invent figures or mistake reported incidents for all crime. Comparisons should use consistent years and offense categories.

Community summary: For recent police activity, consult Topeka Police Department releases. For historical trends and comparable reported-offense rates, consult FBI Crime Data Explorer. Reporting delays and changes in data collection can affect trends.

Violent crime

Includes offenses such as homicide, rape, robbery and aggravated assault in FBI reporting. Rates require official counts and population denominators.

Property crime

Includes offenses such as burglary, larceny-theft and motor vehicle theft. Compare like-for-like categories and periods.

Safety resources

Call 911 for emergencies. For non-emergency reports, use the Topeka Police Department's official contact information.
