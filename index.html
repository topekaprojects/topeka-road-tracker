WHY THIS EXISTS

Built by Joseph Romero, a Topeka resident.

I made this site because I’m tired of popping tires on potholes and being late because of road closures. The goal is simple: make Topeka’s road projects, weather, closures, reporting tools, news and events easier to see in one place. This is an independent community project and is not an official City of Topeka or KDOT website.
